DESIGN & METHODOLOGY

Evidence in context

The navigator uses structured JSON to make individual studies easy to discover and update. Each entry records eligibility, treatment, comparator, primary outcome, available effect estimates, and citation. A Python/Streamlit version adds study-type and outcome filters, comparisons, and guidance records.

01

Traceable sources

Primary trial publications are linked by DOI and PMID, plus ClinicalTrials.gov identifiers when recorded.

02

Explicit denominators

Enrollment criteria and LVEF thresholds remain visible, even when studies span HFmrEF and HFpEF.

03

Outcome-aware summaries

Hazard ratios, recurrent-event rate ratios, and patient-reported outcomes are labeled separately.

Clinical review pending

These first records are source-checked drafts, not an exhaustive review or expert-approved synthesis.
